Welcome on Dev Tools Studio!!

Now Cold Emailing is just only click away, no overhead of re-typing the whole email or copy-pasting previously used emails.
Create customizable email templates, attachments, send emails to others real-time or schedule it for later & send it from your different accounts by adding your SMTP/OAuth Credentials.

  • Get Started in few steps:
  • 01
    Sign Up
    Signup on Dev Tools Studio
  • 02
    Add Credentials (SMTP/OAuth)
    Add credentials to start sending emails from your different accounts
  • 03
    Add Email Templates
    Add email template as per your day to day usage
  • 04
    Add Attachments (Optional)
    Add attachments to save it on cloud (AWS S3 Bucket)!!
  • 05
    Send Email !!
    Choose email template, email account credentials, attachments and timestamp, That's it !!
